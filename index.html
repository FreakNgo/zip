    Privacy Policy

    Your files never leave your device.

    Last updated: August 16, 2026

    At ANTIFY (antifyzip.vercel.app), your privacy is our absolute priority. This policy explains how your data is treated when you use our web-based file compression tool and web utilities.

    01 100% on-device & local processing

    ANTIFY is designed to function as an offline-capable, local web application.

    • No file uploads: when you drag files, folders, or archives into ANTIFY, no data is ever uploaded to any external server, cloud storage, or third-party service.
    • Browser-level compression: all compression (ZIP generation) is executed entirely within your device's browser using local client-side JavaScript. Your files never leave your device.

    02 Information we do NOT collect

    Because ANTIFY operates completely locally on your client device, we do not collect, store, or monitor:

    • The contents, names, or metadata of the files you compress.
    • Your personal identification information (e.g. name, address, email address).
    • User-uploaded assets or temporary directory structures.

    03 Analytics & infrastructure

    Hosting platforms: ANTIFY is hosted on modern web hosting infrastructure (such as Vercel and GitHub Pages). These hosting providers may automatically collect basic server log data (such as IP address, browser type, and access times) strictly for network security, DDoS protection, and performance monitoring.

    External libraries: ANTIFY loads essential fonts (via Google Fonts) and client-side dependencies (such as JSZip) via secure Content Delivery Networks (CDNs). These CDNs serve static scripts to your browser to power the user interface and local ZIP generation.

    04 Direct payments & tips

    If you choose to support the developer via the "Buy me a coffee" feature:

    • All payments are processed directly through external Unified Payments Interface (UPI) deep-link applications (e.g. Google Pay, Paytm, PhonePe) or standard payment gateways.
    • ANTIFY does not store, process, or have access to your bank details, UPI PINs, or financial transaction histories. All financial transactions are governed strictly by the privacy policies of your chosen payment provider.
